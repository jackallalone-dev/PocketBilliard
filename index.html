    01

    Find your angle

    Tap the cloth to aim at a point, or grab and drag the stick to rotate it around the cue ball. Roll the FINE AIM wheel up or down for tiny adjustments. The dotted line shows your ball’s path.

    02

    Pull, then release

    Pull down on the side power control. A longer pull means more power. Release to shoot. Slide back up or move sideways to cancel. Choose the left or right side in Settings.

    03

    Save the 8 for last

    The first colored ball pocketed assigns solids or stripes. Clear your seven, then pocket the 8 to win. Sink it early or scratch on that shot, and the other player wins.

    Pocket your own ball to keep your turn; otherwise pass the device. A scratch ends your turn and returns the cue ball to a clear spot. Groups are assigned only on a scratch-free shot. No called pockets or first-contact fouls. On desktop, use ← / → to aim, ↑ / ↓ for power, and Space to shoot.
    Playground: choose Playground from the main menu. In Arrange, drag a ball or its offset handle, or use ＋ Balls to select a ball to place. Switch to Shoot to practice without turns or 8-ball penalties. Retry setup restores your saved arrangement. Open the menu by tapping the Pocket Club logo.
